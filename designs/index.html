Designs

Design decision notes for Evaluchat platform features. These documents record the rationale, constraints, and agreed mechanics for a feature before implementation — design intent, not shipped product truth. A design graduates to a concepts/ specification once the feature ships.

Design Status What it covers
Evidence publishing mechanics draft · graduated Evidence definition as an integral part of a Method, the workspace Evidence action, and the submit-to-PR filing flow into the research catalog — shipped; see Evidence contribution concept
Finding authoring — evidence ledgers and human-controlled claims draft On-demand immutable evidence ledgers, freshness detection, and a human-only finding canvas with read-only AI advice
