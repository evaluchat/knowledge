# Welcome to Evaluchat

This is your private workspace for thinking, writing, and working with AI.
The Markdown document is yours: edit it freely, keep the parts that help, and
replace the rest with your own work.

## Try asking

- What can I do in this workspace?
- Help me turn an idea into an outline.
- Explain something I am working on.
- Review this draft and suggest a next step.

You can also start writing directly in the document. Ask the assistant for
help whenever you want it.
